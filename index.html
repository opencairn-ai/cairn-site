The opinionated, sovereign,
open source AI stack

Model capability isn't the bottleneck.
Deployment is.

Every enterprise, foundation, and mission-driven organization we talk to is trying to reassemble the same AI stack in-house. Model server, inference, retrieval, memory, orchestration, governance, access control, observability.

Everyone is doing this work.
With unnecessary challenges. In isolation. Repeatedly. And rebuilding it every six months as the ecosystem changes underneath them.

Cairn is a research project to design and prove out that stack, opinionated and open.

Put concretely: your own frontier AI, running on your own machines. Your laptop, your data centre, your sovereign compute — one command to install, your data under your control, with governance and access control built in from the start, not bolted on after the demo.

Cairn is a collaboration between Mila, one of the world's leading AI research institutes, and Mozilla, the mission-driven organization behind Firefox and the broader open web.

Cairn is designed to graduate. Open source from day one, evolving with the ecosystem that helps build it. Mozilla's role here is to shape and seed.
