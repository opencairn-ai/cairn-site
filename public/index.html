The opinionated, sovereign,
open source AI stack.

Model capability isn't the bottleneck.
Deployment is.

The last two years produced the models. The next two need to produce the stacks people can actually run them on. Cairn is a research collaboration between Mozilla and Mila to build one of those stacks. Opinionated, sovereign, open source.

An opinionated, coordinated collection of open source components that snap together into a system a team can actually deploy: model server, inference, retrieval, memory, orchestration, agent evaluation, harness, governance, access control, observability.

Cairn is trying to be for open source AI what Ubuntu was for Linux: not a new operating system, a curated, opinionated way of running one.

Put concretely: your own frontier AI, running on your own machines. Your laptop, your data centre, your sovereign compute. One command to install, your data under your control, with governance and access control built in from the start, not bolted on after the demo.

Cairn is a collaboration between Mila, one of the world's leading AI research institutes, and Mozilla, the mission-driven organization behind Firefox and the broader open web. Mozilla's role here is to invest, shape and steward. Mila leads the technical work.
